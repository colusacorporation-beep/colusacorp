About COLUSA

Built to Move Latin Products Forward

COLUSA BY JP CORP is a U.S.-based importer and distributor focused on Latin American food and beverage products. We connect trusted suppliers with businesses across the U.S. market.

Our approach combines sourcing, import coordination, warehousing and wholesale distribution with a strong focus on service and long-term customer relationships.
